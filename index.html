Timing and route assumptions Days start at 08:00 local time. Times estimate movement, excluding personal breaks and transport waits. Faster uses approximately 14% less time than Normal. Clockwise timings are approximate reverse-direction estimates. These presets follow the supplied route via Bovine and the Chamonix-side finish, not a full loop back to the start. Bellevue, Plan Chécrouit and Planpraz sections may assume lifts; the Glaciers valley may involve a shuttle. Check service availability and allow extra time if walking. Weather windows start on the hour, snapping forward when the next hour is within 15 minutes. Ends round up to the hour, with a minimum two-hour window. Overlapping windows are intentional.
